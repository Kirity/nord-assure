The challenge

Small teams. Critical systems. Real exposure.

Engineering and service businesses depend on remote access, project files and third-party systems. Most don’t have a dedicated cyber team to verify the protections in place.

Customer requirements keep growing

Security questions appear in tenders, supplier reviews and technical documentation. It is not always clear what evidence is actually needed.

01 / CUSTOMER EXPECTATIONS

IT and operational systems overlap

Remote support, shared networks, engineering workstations and suppliers create risks that generic office-IT checklists can miss.

02 / TECHNICAL EXPOSURE

Too many risks, no clear priority

Even when issues are known, management needs to understand what matters first, why it matters and what improvement should look like.

How we work

From uncertainty to a plan.

A clearly agreed scope, an evidence-led review, and a prioritized improvement plan. No intrusive testing unless specifically authorized.

01

Understand

We learn how your business operates, which systems matter and what is driving the review—customer requirements, operational risks or both.

02

03
