The challenge

Small teams. Critical systems. Real exposure.

Engineering and service businesses depend on remote access, project files and third-party systems. Most don’t have a dedicated cyber team to verify the protections in place.

Customer requirements keep growing

Security questions appear in tenders, supplier reviews and technical documentation. It is not always clear what evidence is actually needed.

01 / CUSTOMER EXPECTATIONS

IT and operational systems overlap

Remote support, shared networks, engineering workstations and suppliers create risks that generic office-IT checklists can miss.

02 / TECHNICAL EXPOSURE

Too many risks, no clear priority

Even when issues are known, management needs to understand what matters first, why it matters and what improvement should look like.

A clear assessment scope

What we review. What you receive.

A narrowly scoped, non-intrusive starting point designed for smaller engineering businesses. Any technical access, scans or production-system changes require separate written authorization.

01

Understand

Short discussion of business-critical systems, engineering processes, remote support and supplier access.

02

Review

Relevant diagrams, access procedures, backup evidence, ownership records and incident arrangements.

03

Prioritize

Risk-ranked findings, limitations, recommended control owners and practical first actions.

04

Explain

Plain-language management briefing, technical appendix and a discussion of next steps.

Excluded by default: penetration testing, intrusive OT scanning, continuous monitoring and certification audits. These are never implied by the baseline assessment.
The deliverable

An actionable report—not another checklist.

See the findings, evidence gaps and next steps in one place. The goal is a report the business owner and technical team can both act on.

  • Concise executive overview of key risks
  • Evidence and clear scope boundaries
  • Prioritized improvements with practical next steps

Priority improvements

Example of how findings can translate into manageable actions.

HIGH
Review and restrict third-party remote access

Confirm who can connect, how access is approved, and how it is disabled after work.

HIGH
Evidence an actual recovery test

Schedule a restore exercise for a critical system and document the result.

MEDIUM
Assign incident-response responsibilities

Document a clear escalation path and key contacts.

How we work

From uncertainty to a plan.

A clearly agreed scope, an evidence-led review, and a prioritized improvement plan. No intrusive testing unless specifically authorized.

01

Understand

We learn how your business operates, which systems matter and what is driving the review—customer requirements, operational risks or both.

02

Assess

We review agreed documents, controls and evidence. Any technical verification is specifically scoped and authorized before it begins.

03

Prioritize

We explain the findings and agree on realistic next steps, starting with the measures that reduce meaningful risk.
